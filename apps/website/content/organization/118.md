PyData Singapore is a group for users and developers of data analysis tools to share ideas and learn from each other. We gather to discuss how best to apply Python tools, as well as those using R and Julia, to meet the evolving challenges in data management, processing, analytics, and visualization. PyData Singapore is supported by NumFOCUS.org, a 501(c)3 non-profit in the United States.

The PyData Code of Conduct governs this meetup. To discuss any issues or concerns relating to the code of conduct or the behavior of anyone at a PyData meetup, please contact the co-organizer: Eugene Teo (eugeneteo[0x40]gmailcom).
