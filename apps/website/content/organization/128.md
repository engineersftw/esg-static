Onemaker Group (OMG) is a unique consortium comprising the key organizations of the Maker Movement in Singapore.

OneMaker Group (OMG) aims to become a nexus for people to discover new dimensions of themselves through Making. We are proud to support and cultivate designers, engineers, entrepreneurs, students and the Maker community by setting up welcoming Makerspaces, offering a comprehensive Maker curriculum, Design programs as well as Product Development consulting which unifies Making, Design and Entrepreneurship.

We currently operate the Prototyping Lab@National Design Centre which provides and hosts a variety of workshops such as 3D printing, CNC Milling, Laser Cutting, Traditional Hand-Tools Crafting and many more. These are made accessible for the public to make, invent, prototype and explore without boundaries.

OMG is backed by the key players in Singapore’s Maker Movement - Simplifi3D, Home-Fix, Sustainable Living Lab (SL2), Focustech Ventures, SmartSpace and Hyperflow Group. We are also supported by SPRING Singapore and the DesignSingapore Council.
