The IxDA (Interaction Design Association) Singapore group aims to support interaction design professionals or anyone in the relevant field to find opportunities, network and socialize with your professional peers. We like to foster a community where meaningful exchange of ideas and knowledge can take place.

We're welcome any businesses / companies which are either practicing the IxD discipline or are seeking professionals in the field to join us in the goal of making better products and services for users.
