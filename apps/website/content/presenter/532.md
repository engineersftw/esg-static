I am currently a Program Manager for the Google Cloud Platform.  My mission is to democratize machine learning so that it can be done by anyone anywhere  using Google's amazing infrastructure (i.e., without deep knowledge of statistics or programming or ownership of lots of hardware). 

Previously as a Director at the Climate Corporation, I led a team of data scientists (statisticians, engineers, meteorologists) who build probabilistic estimates of past, current and future weather. Before that, I was a Senior Research Scientist at CIMMS/U. Oklahoma/National Severe Storms Laboratory. My Google Scholar page captures the ways in which that work is used by other scientists.
