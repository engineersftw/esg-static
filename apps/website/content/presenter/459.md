After graduating in Visual Communication in France, Maxime worked as a producer supervising design projects for many renown TV stations such as MTV, Eurosport, Fox Kids and international Brands like Cartier, HP, Pernod Ricard, L'oreal, Levi's, Huawei, Armani, Nike. He has spent 8 years in Shanghai, China as Creative Producer collaborating with many international agencies on brand events and video productions. In 2013, he moved to Singapore and joined Eon Reality, where he has been continuously growing his passion for VR.
