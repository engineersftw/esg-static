Just a really curious techie who doesn't like to be pigeonholed and is always finding ways to connect with people and places surrounding her community in tech, innovation and passion for societal issues. One of the fun things I do is hosting a tech podcast called ragTech, talking about the realities of life in tech together with my two other techie friends, and with guest speakers from peers all around!

I also lead collaborations and partnerships for my beloved women in tech community, Women Devs SG, which was formerly Women Who Code Singapore before it dissolved.

Professionally, I specialize in building 0-1 teams and products, mostly web apps, and establishing lean and agile practices within small teams that can be scaled.
