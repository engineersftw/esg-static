Before switching career paths, Clara was an entrepreneur running her own fashion business. Realising at 30 that she no longer had interest in staying in the fashion industry, she decided to start pursuing her interest in tech instead. Armed with only a basic understanding of HTML/CSS, she found out about TechLadies’ bootcamp programme and decided to submit an application. She graduated from the 10-week bootcamp in December 2016, and has since transitioned to a full-time software engineer with a local startup.
