Audrey Roy Greenfeld is co-author of the Two Scoops of Django series, creator and core developer of Cookiecutter, and wife of Daniel Roy Greenfeld. She leads a number of open source projects, including Django Packages, cookiecutter-pypackage, and more. She teaches Django at Two Scoops Academy and is a principal at Cartwheel Web. When she’s not working, she enjoys watercolor painting, sculpture, and photography.
