Arun is a data engineer at ThoughtWorks. He is a curious and passionate engineer with extensive experience in the architecture and development of highly scalable distributed applications using Scala, Hadoop, Akka and Spark. He is the author of "Scala Data Analysis Cookbook" and co-author of "Scala: Guide for Data Science Professionals", which focuses on Spark, Spark ML and Hadoop for solving various analytics problems.
