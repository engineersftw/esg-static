Chinab is the co-founder of Jublia which specializes in business matching at events with real-time data analytics. Apart from leading the development team, Chinab works on moonshot ideas that pushes the boundaries of interfacing technology with face-to-face interactions. He was born in India and brought up in Indonesia, having a diverse background within Asia. Chinab has graduated from the National University of Singapore, with a Bachelor of Engineering with Honours and University Scholars Programme. Outside of the office, Chinab enjoys playing tennis, runs several web projects on the side and is fascinated by new technology research.
