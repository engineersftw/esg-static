Sau Sheong has been doing software development for 24 years, mostly in web application development for various industries including telcos, fintech, gaming, government and also energy/utility. He is active in the Ruby and Go developer communities and has contributed to open source projects and spoke at meetups and conferences. Sau Sheong has also published 4 programming-related books, on Ruby and Go, his latest being Go Web Programming published by Manning. He currently works for Singapore Power (a utility company), and has in his career worked for PayPal, HP, Yahoo, and also ran a technology startup during the dot-com days.
