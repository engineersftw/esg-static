Carl is the CEO of Zenika, an IT Consulting and Training company which he co-founded in 2006, after being a software developer for most of his career. Zenika now has close to 500 employees spread across 9 offices in France, Singapore and Canada. Carl wants to disrupt the model of the IT companies and leads the « Coding the World » project. He is passionate about topics such as Innovation, Professional open source, and work-life balance in the Corporate world.
