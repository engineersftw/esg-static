Jon is a developer at Stack Overflow since 2013 and heads up Marketing Engineering and Developer Evangelism there. He is also the founder of Bento, a site that helps hundreds of thousands of self-taught developers around the world learn to code from the best resources. Jon speaks frequently on tech education, diversity, and how to build engineering focused startups. Before Stack Overflow and Bento, Jon was a technology consultant at Deloitte and graduated from New York University.
