As a software-engineer-turned-product-manager-turned-entrepreneur, Vish's career has revolved around designing and building products and solutions that aim to solve real-world challenges. He currently leads product and engineering at Split, a young Singapore-born startup that aims to make travel more accessible by enabling travellers to pay for their trips in instalments. Before that, Vish led a team of software engineers and designers at O2O platform Fave, to develop and launch their FavePay and FaveBiz products. In his free time, he works on IoT projects, mentors young entrepreneurs, and binges on Dragon Ball Z reruns.
