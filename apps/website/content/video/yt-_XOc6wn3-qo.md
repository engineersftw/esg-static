You are in the beginning of 2026. 2025 has been brutal on you in terms of keeping you on the edge of trying to make you learn everything possible about AI. Your brain CPU overclocked and disk has been fragmented quite a lot by hearing and learning multiple things about AI. You are confused how to bring all of this accumulation of knowledge and skills to use in practicality. Should you fail to bring this defragmentation - too many bad sectors get created in your mind and hampers your ability to learn in the future and wonder why despite all the skills and knowledge you fail to grow and frustration sets in.

In this talk, Pradeep Soundararajan, who sees the world from the moon and observes himself and others as a tiny spec on a beautiful ball with the drama of AI that has been happening - will present simple, coherent thoughts on de-fragmenting AI in Testing.

As an example - he will speak about the difference between learning AI to build something versus learning AI to operate it to solving a problem. Knowing this difference will free up a lot of people. He wishes a lot more people get this message because there are immensely talented people in this world of testing but they all seem to roam around like zombies heading (learning) in all directions (whatever it thrown at them to learn) without a clarity as to why they are heading that way. At least that's how it looks from the moon.
