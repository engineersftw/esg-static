This talk is about a rubygem called aka - a shortcut to manage terminal shortcut for the productivity and happiness of developers.

aka improves your workflow and give you more time to focus on the things that matter.

aka started as a weekend project and became open source in 2015. aka was created while on a travel trip to Ho Chi Minh and Penang.

This talk was recorded at GeekCampSG 2015 on 26 September 2015.
