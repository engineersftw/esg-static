Speaker: Pavlos Daniel, CMS Architect, SGX

During this session we will go through the GraphQL contrib module.

- We will briefly talk about GraphQL.
- We will touch on why GraphQL vs a pure Rest API.
- The major features of the module.
- How the module was used to help us create and decouple the new SGX content repository which powers (as a start) the new SGX website.
