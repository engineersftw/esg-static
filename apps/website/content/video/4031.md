Speaker: Kennard Wicoady

In modern era, Optical Character Recognition (OCR) usage is more frequent, especially to increase user experience factor. Such as scanning user’s card’s number or identity card and automatically fill it to form, without user’s direct interaction. Let’s deep down OCR’s origin, how it works, how it creates pattern and learns it to scan, and implement it into Golang-based system.
