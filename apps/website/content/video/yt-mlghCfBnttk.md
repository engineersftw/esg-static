Software testing is undergoing a seismic shift. Automation, AI, Agile and DevOps have accelerated delivery, yet many organizations continue to measure testing through archaic lenses.

In this talk, I bring out a new Testing Value Equation that redefines quality as a multiplier of trust, speed and business acumen.

The RISE framework (Reimagine, Integrate, Shift, Elevate) can help teams embed quality earlier, smarter and collaboratively.
