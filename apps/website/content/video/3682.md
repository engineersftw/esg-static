"Building Inclusive Apps in React Native
Speaker: Apurva Jain

One key aspect of building a robust app is ensuring that anyone can use it, including people with disabilities. React Native enables you to build inclusive and accessible apps!
Join me to see a working demo of how to design, develop, and test for accessibility in the mobile space. Talk covers the following:
What is Accessibility?
Who is it for?
Design for Accessibility
Code for Accessibility

Dear Developer, It’s up to you to ensure that what you create is usable and accessible for as many people as possible."
