Speaker: Xin Tian, Graduate of TechLadies Bootcamp

A lot of developers see the gender imbalance in the tech community and are actively volunteering their time and resources to teach more women how to code. In this talk, I will share my experiences learning how to code through the TechLadies Bootcamp, what challenges I’ve faced, and how you can help newbies pick up Ruby and Rails.

Speaker's Bio
Xin Tian is a Masters dropout, customer service rep, and a graduate from the TechLadies Bootcamp. She now also co-organises the TechLadies Tech Talks and TechLadies Code Pairadise, providing a newbie-friendly environment for women to learn coding.
