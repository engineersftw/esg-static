Speaker: Marc Juul

What are the major barriers to open collaboration in biology within the areas of wetware, software and lawware and how is the BioBricks Foundation providing solutions and workarounds + bonus update from Counter Culture Labs.

Marc Juul hails from Oakland, California. He is a  co-founder and co-organizer of the hackerspace sudoroom.org , the biohackerspace counterculturelabs.org and the radical community space omnicommons org . He is currently working on the decentralized biology sharing platform bionet.io , the wireless community mesh network peoplesopen.net and the open source ebook reader operating system fread.ink . Marc is lead software developer at biobricks.org and previously co-founded labitat.dk
