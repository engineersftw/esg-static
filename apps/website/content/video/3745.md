Speaker: Austin Freel

Hi there, my name is Austin and I've been a SWE at Facebook for nearly 3 years now where I've worked on things like VR, Groups, and Ads. Before joining full-time, I interned at Facebook (twice), and during one of my internships I built the rainbow pride profile picture filter at a hackathon. My talk is about that hackathon, the experience of going from late-night idea to global launch in 72 hours, and the lessons I learned along the way that have made me a better engineer.
