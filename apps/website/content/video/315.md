Grace Huang, Founder & Creative Director (in martial arts)

Grace isn’t someone you will want to mess around with, and you’ll only need to see her business card to know why - She is one half of the dynamic duo behind Trifecta Martial Arts, and is trained in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (with medals under that, no less), Chinese Wushu and Muay Thai.

Besides fighting, gruelling workouts, and the occasional extreme meal plans to keep in tip-top condition, this tough nut is pretty much a slave to her Macbook(s), whipping up creative design work as a freelance web/graphic designer and art director. Her work includes various projects for big-name brands and corporations including Ogilvy & Mather (Singapore), Mercedes Benz, and the Ritz Carlton Hotel (Singapore)… to name a few.

In other words, she’s also a nerd in disguise. Looks are always deceiving, aren’t they?
