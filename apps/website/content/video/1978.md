Speaker: Alan Hsu, general partner & co-founder of KK Fund

Alan is one of the judges of Startup-UP, a competition hosted on Channel News Asia for 4 seasons. Before his days as a VC at GREE Ventures, Alan started out as a management consultant at McKinsey & Co. in United States and also was at Goldman Sachs New York as investment banker driving tech, media and telco merger and acquisitions. Moving to Singapore, he worked at private equity for Temasek Holdings.
