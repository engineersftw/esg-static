Speaker: Open Source Panel

We all use open source software in our personal, business or work related projects. Ever wondered who the people behind developing & maintaining these open source libraries are?
Join us on for a panel discussion to know more about the intricacies of open source development & learn from the masters themselves. Hear them share their career journeys, their motivations behind contributing to OS and how they manage teams on various OS projects.

Our panelists:
1) Juergen Hoeller – Co-founder & Project lead for Spring Framework
2) Emmanuel Bernard – Chief Architect Red Hat, Java champion and open source contributor on Hibernate ORM
3) David Pilato – Developer and Evangelist at Elastic
4) Kohsuke Kawaguchi – Creator of Jenkins & CTO CloudBees
This panel will be moderated by Purnima Kamath – Director at Women Who Code SG & Evangelist at YOW!

Event Website: https://voxxeddays.com/singapore/
