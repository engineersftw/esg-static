Speaker: Danilo Sato (@dtsato)

DevOps and Continuous Delivery practices help increase deployment frequency of systems to production, while also increasing their reliability. A focus on automation makes it possible to deploy several times a day. However, operations teams might still resist when you try to apply these practices. In this talk I will present some principles and deployment practices that can help reduce the risk with frequent deployments and discuss how to increase collaboration between the development and operations teams. 

About the Speaker:

Danilo Sato is a Principal Consultant at Thoughtworks with more than 15 years of experience as developer, architect, Agile coach, trainer, data engineer, and devops guru. Currently, Danilo is a member of the Office of the CTO, and the global initiative lead for Data at ThoughtWorks. He helps clients adopt practices to reduce the time between having an idea, implementing it and running it in production using Cloud, DevOps and Continuous Delivery. Danilo is the author of “Devops in Practice: reliable and automated software delivery”, co-host of the Mind the Cloud podcast, and an international conference speaker having presented talks and workshops in conferences such as QCon, RubyConf, AgileBrazil, Agile, and XP
