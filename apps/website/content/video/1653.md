Speaker: Herdy Handoko

Dotty is the next-generation Scala compiler currently in active development. In this  presentation, we will learn about what Dotty is, its current state, developer tooling support, and the plans for the future. 

About the speaker:

Herdy is a software engineer with a keen interest in web technologies and distributed systems. After working for almost a decade exclusively in the .NET ecosystem, he discovered Scala and migrated from sunny Perth, Australia last year to take up a full-time Scala role in Singapore. So far, he's enjoying fast gigabit internet, vibrant tech community, and the various culinary delights Singapore has to offer.
