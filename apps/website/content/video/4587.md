What happens when AI stops being a tool… and starts becoming an extension of how you think and work?
This session showcases personal AI agents built using Openclaw and NanoClaw, demonstrating how designed systems can extend cognition, reduce friction, and reshape personal daily workflows.

The presenters will help you understand what AI agents, OpenClaw, and NanoClaw actually are. By seeing real examples, you'll walk away with a practical map of how to automate your own daily life and workflows

About Victoria:
I'm a solutions engineer and technical writer for over 5 years, and WomenDevs Singapore Director.

About Zawanah:
I was a Senior Business Analytics Partner driving analytics programmes across 5 markets in EMEA/APAC, and now design AI-assisted workflows and personal agents for my day-to-day.

Zawanah's segment will be on NanoClaw covering: Why she chose NanoClaw as a non-software engineer, key architecture differences, lessons learnt from building her agents to bring forward, demo setup, my use cases - personal assistant, home manager, journaling, career navigator, then she will close with using agents to make our lives easier.
