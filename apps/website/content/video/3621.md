About TechLadies Bootcamp #5

The TechLadies Bootcamp is a 12-week part-time accelerated learning program designed to help women with some basic programming background become professional programmers. Ladies are guided by industry experts to create products for nonprofit organizations.
30% of the bootcamp graduates successfully get roles in software engineering, product management, UX/UI design.

About The Inaugural TechLadies Mentorship

TechLadies Mentorship is a 3-month mentorship program to provide women with the career guidance as they chart their paths in the tech industry. Women will be matched individually to industry practitioners with backgrounds in software engineering, product management, and UI/UX design.
