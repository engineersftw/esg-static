Durable Functions is an extension of Azure Functions and Azure WebJobs that lets you write stateful functions in a serverless environment. The extension manages state, checkpoints, and restarts for you.

He has always been excited about the potential of technology to make the world a better place. He is currently the Software Engineer from Microsoft Asia Pacific. Aside from this, Kenneth has been actively mentoring students and aspiring software engineers to become the next generation technical leader especially through thought leadership in technology. Kenneth has a strong background in Azure, specializing in Modern Applications and Serverless Computing with experience in Machine Learning.
