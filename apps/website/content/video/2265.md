Han and Ankush will take us through their journey to building an Unbounded Disk-Based Cache and will focus on the following points:
- How they used MappedByteBuffers and linux mmap() features to build a low-latency and high throughput cache that doesn't expire records
- Problems they faced along the way and how they tackled them
