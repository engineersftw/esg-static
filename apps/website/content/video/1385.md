"At Google, women make up 30 percent of the company's overall workforce, but hold only 17 percent of the company's tech jobs. At Facebook, 15 percent of tech roles are staffed by women. At Twitter, it's a laughable 10 percent. For non-technical jobs at Twitter (think marketing, HR, sales), the gender split is 50-50." 

You've asked for it. You've wanted to know more. We've put it together. We're bringing together some of the best known women in Singapore's technology space representing Accenture, Uber, VC Firm - SoGal & IMDA/ Gov Tech, to provide you with some insight on maneouvering the technology landscape, how to be successful and how to enter it. Have any burning questions - let us know!
