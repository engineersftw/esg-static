Presented at Hackatron Asia 2014 (http://hackatron.techinasia.com)

Speaker: Anna Filippova, co-organizer of Ruby Group and Rails Girls

Ruby is a dynamic, reflective, object-oriented, general-purpose programming language. It was designed and developed in the mid-1990s. Ruby on Rails, or simply, Rails, is an open source web application framework written in Ruby.
Come by and get “30 Random Ruby Tips” from Winston. Also hear from Anna Filippova, a co-organizer of Singapore Ruby User Group and a PhD candidate who does research on online communities. She’ll be speaking on “Build it and convince them to come: tips on seeding and growing your community. Lessons learnt from the Ruby community”.
