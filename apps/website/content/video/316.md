Ayla Kremb, Coach and Customer Experience Designer

"I help entrepreneurs give Love @ Scale.
Splitting my childhood between Sweden, Germany, Denmark and the USA, I've always found people fascinating.

With my knack for anticipating human needs I started in sales at Ralph Lauren, then managed guest relations for the Ritz Carlton San Francisco.

Arriving in Singapore relaunched award-winning micro-business school Aidha's curriculum, before joining the Hub as their head business coach and now leading Customer Service for robotics startup Zimplistic.

Having worked with over 250 entrepreneurs, from fitness studio owners to luxury shoe retailers, I love creating amazing customer experiences.

In my free time I read, write copy and chat with entrepreneurs."
