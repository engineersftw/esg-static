Speaker: Adrian Tan

Adrian Tan is the co-founder and former Managing Director of RecruitPlus Consulting. He has spent a decade in recruitment was chairing the national recruitment association. Along the way, he co-authored a career guide book 'Everything You Wish to Ask a Headhunter'. He now does career training and career coaching under CareerLadder. He is married and have two sons and one daughter. More about him at www.adriantan.com.sg.
