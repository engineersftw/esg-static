Speaker: Hon Cheng and Junjie 

Hon Cheng and Junjie won the very 1st Apple Design Awards (ADA) for Singapore and Southeast Asia for their app Elk.  Hon Cheng makes things happen, and makes them really fast, and he obsesses himself with animation. He made some of the most popular apps in Singapore—SG Buses and SG NextBus. Junjie pays attention to how things work, how fast and how easy a user can do something. His own app and foray into app development—Due—was featured on Daring Fireball. ​

After many sessions of badminton and bubble tea, they founded Clean Shaven Apps in June 2012. Together, they have launched six apps. Their most recent app, Elk, won an Apple Design Award in 2017.
