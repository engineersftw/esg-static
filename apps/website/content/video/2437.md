Speaker: Adarsh S
Info: https://2018.fossasia.org/event/speakers.html#adarsh-s3053

While he was working on Open Source Linguistic tools at ICFOSS under the mentorship of Dr Rajeev R.R on multiple occasions he heard people saying "Hey Adarsh wouldn't it be nice if something like 'INSERT NLP TOOL HERE' was available for 'INSERT NOT SO POPULAR LANGUAGE HERE'". So here he'll explore how to potentially help a LOT of people (LOT is an understatement) by building simple NLP tools without reinventing the wheel and also getting world class Machine Learning engineers as a by-product.
