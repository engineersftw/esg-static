The time has come for our first Talks evening! We are happy to be back at ThoughtWorks for this one, now at their new and spacious office at China Sq Central. So make sure to get a friend or colleague curious about Elm, and invite them to this event!

The plan as it currently looks:
19.00 - Arrive and say hi
19.10 - How to Get Started with Elm, by Mads
19.40 - Using Tests in Elm (current board leader), by Matthieu
20.10 - PureScript - another JavaScript-hosted strongly typed language like Elm, by Michal from Jewel Paymentech Pte Ltd
20.40 - Q&A / informal discussions
21.00 - See you next time

If you want to have a say on which talk Matthieu will being doing, cast your vote on the open poll. Do you have comments on the planning or do you want do a talk? Head on over to our planning "issue" on Github.
