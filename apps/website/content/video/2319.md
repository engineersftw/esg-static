The mental well-being of working adults in Singapore is 13% lower than that of the general population and almost 10% of Singapore Residents will struggle with at least one mood or anxiety disorder at some point in their adult life. But even though we are talking about mental health more than ever before, the common perception exists that those with mental illness could get better if they only wanted to and that their condition is a sign of personal weakness.

This month, SheSays Singapore and expert guests will discuss living strong, turning vulnerability into an asset and creating a workplace culture that promotes mental health.

Our speakers this month include the fabulous:

Marianne Blamire, Managing Directer, MullenLowe Salt
Elizabeth Piper, Occupactional Therapist, Lifeweavers
Tan Teng Nging, Life Coach and Therapist, Wonder Full People
Meera Jane Navaratnam, Digital & Innovation, Accenture

Moderated by Alisha Tukol and Marta Szatanke
