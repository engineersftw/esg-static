And we have double meetup in May! In this meetup, we have Chiu Hansheng, Head of Data, from ReFUEL4 to share more with us on using deep learning in online advertising. Using artificial intelligence, ReFUEL4 provides partners and designers live campaign data that guides the further development of ads. Advertising will never be the same!
