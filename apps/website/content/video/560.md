Speaker: Sangeeta Misra

Sangeeta Misra - As Business Development Manager at Ranosys Technologies Pte. Ltd., one of Singapore's leading e-commerce solutions provider, Sangeeta focuses on Magento. She specializes in enabling brick-and-mortar retailers to sell online by deploying Magento based e-commerce stores for them, assisting existing e-commerce players to migrate from other platforms to Magento and supporting existing Magento e-commerce users to better exploit the many benefits of Magento by introducing them to new features and possibilities that this platform has to offer. As the account manager of some of the biggest e-commerce portals in Singapore, Sangeeta leverages her knowledge of e-commerce domain and real-time business scenarios to translate business needs into technological requirements for clients.
