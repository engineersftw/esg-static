Speaker: Balaji Venkatesan

Balaji Venkatesan is currently heading product development for Zuellig Pharma Patient solutions. He is a staunch believer in the transformational nature of technology. In this talk, Balaji will give a view into the many areas in which exponential technology is revolutionizing healthcare.
