Speaker: Darvin Kurniawan, CEO and Co-founder, REIDAO

In the past few months fundraising activities through "ICO" has been on the rise especially for blockchain based startups. We will explore a new way of fundraising structure using token that is backed by real asset to protect participants interest, and generally make it safer.

About the speaker

Darvin is the CEO and Co-founder of Singapore-based blockchain startup REIDAO. REIDAO is creating digital assets backed by real estate, opening up a new way in getting exposure to real estate opportunities.
