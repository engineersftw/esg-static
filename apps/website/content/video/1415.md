Speaker: Surya Dharma Tio (DevOps Lead, Mohammad Aslam (DevOps Engineer) 

When talking about companies that deploy their applications to production several times a day, we always hear of the same Silicon Valley examples: Google, Netflix, Facebook...

We're glad to have a few companies in Singapore that are doing it just as well. RedMart has moved to Continuous Delivery and a full micro-services architecture a few years ago. 


Surya Dharma Tio (DevOps Lead) and Mohammad Aslam (DevOps Engineer) will walk you through the entire CI/CD pipeline at RedMart, from idea to production rollout.
