Speaker: Renaud Caron (Zenika)

Renaud is a senior consultant for Zenika Singapore, currently acting as a technical lead at SGX.

For the past 10 years he has been working Paris, London and Singapore, building software for investment banks and finance startups. He also delivers trainings in Asia on Spring, React and software development best practices. As a full-stack developer, he is passionate about all kinds of technologies: machine learning, Kotlin, Docker, Vue.js, and Kafka, among others.

Build powerful chatbots with Natural Language Processing

How do computers process and analyze human languages?
Natural Language Processing is the field at the intersection of AI, linguistics and computer science.

Based on professional experience, we will explore the different techniques and tools at the service of NLP. And we will see how we can apply them in order to build a domain-specific chatbot.
