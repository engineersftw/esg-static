Speaker: Björn Andersson

Björn has recently learned about Type Hints (see https://www.python.org/dev/peps/pep-0484/) and discovered that they can be of tremendous help when refactoring large code-bases. He will show us how you can make use of them today, by integrating them into your IDE and gradually including them into your codebase, one module at a time.
