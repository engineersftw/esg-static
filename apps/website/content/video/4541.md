Description:
AI is transforming industries and reshaping how we build applications, making it an essential area for developers to explore. In this Tech Talk, we will delve into the process of building AI-based applications, starting with OpenAI API and progressing into the LangChain framework, which simplifies complex workflows and integrations. Through discussions, a live demo, and insights into LangChain, we’ll uncover how this powerful tool is streamlining AI development.

About Hana Fatinah
Hana is a Software Engineer with a diverse background, starting in data analysis before transitioning into UI/UX design and eventually moving into full-stack development. A graduate of Yale-NUS College, she has won multiple awards in hackathons and datathons across Singapore and Indonesia. With experience in industries such as logistics, edtech, and e-commerce, Hana is passionate about web development and applies test-driven development (TDD) in her daily work to build robust and scalable applications.
