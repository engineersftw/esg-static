As AI transforms the QA landscape, testers must evolve their skillsets to stay relevant. This talk explores how different QA roles can adapt and thrive by embracing AI as a powerful ally rather than a threat.

Key Takeaways - Modern QA Team Composition:

• Domain Expert QAs: Leverage AI to reduce repetitive work and focus on high-value testing

• Automation Testers: Use AI for intelligent script generation and coverage analysis

• ETL/Data Testers: Ensure clean, structured data pipelines and prevent test data loss

• QA Leaders: Champion AI adoption, measure time savings, and build resilient, self-sufficient teams
