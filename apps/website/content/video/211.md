Speaker: Grace Sai

Grace Sai is the CEO and Co-Founder of The Hub Singapore. An ecosystem builder, she is a node for social entrepreneurship in Asia and speaks widely on that topic. She's shared her inputs with Singapore Prime Minister’s Office, and is now advising the Malaysian government on a national social entrepreneurship strategy. She's worked alongside some of the pioneers of the social entrepreneurship and impact investing movement globally. She has to ski and dive at least once a year.

Fuckup Nights is a global movement to share business failure stories in a candid, irreverent way -- Fuckup Nights (FUN) was born in Mexico in 2012. It’s grown a hundred fold since.
