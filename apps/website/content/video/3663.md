There are four types of in-app purchases in Apple's App Store, with their own unique ways to manage it. There is also the challenge of isolating the intricacies of these types from the rest of the application. Otherwise a move from non-consumable in-app purchase to a subscription type would involve a major rewrite. Learn how to manage an app's inventory of purchases, get the code base resilient to changes in the business side, and tips on how to implement some more esoteric business models as in-app purchases.
