Speaker: Abhinav Suryavanshi & Douglas Vaz - GoJek

Go makes it fun to build concurrent software due to its goroutines and channels primitives. In this talk, we will explore patterns that treat channels as a means of communication and use these semantics to orchestrate code.

About our speakers:
Abhinav Suryavanshi & Douglas Vaz are both product engineers working at GoJek, with focus on financial services.
