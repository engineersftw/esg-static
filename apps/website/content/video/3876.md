Speaker: James Yong

Over the past 6 years I've been helping friends prepare for technical interviews with purely technical exercises. Just a year ago I had to go through job-hunting myself and decided to thoroughly analyze various non-technical aspects throughout my journey.

This (intense) session with both mini-exercises and stories would serve as both a motivational driver and informative session for attendees. This is also the first time I would share this.

Rough outline:
==============
- Impostor Syndrome
- Depression
- Finding Purpose
- Psych
- Personal Goals
- Company Culture
- Negotiation
- Acceptance
