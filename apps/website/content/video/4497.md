Ville Nordström from Coda Payments

"Early product decisions, or knowing when to try a wrong turn - Case Codashop"

Ville is a seasoned product grunt, currently with Coda Payments in Singapore, making it easier for people in SEA to conveniently spend their money online. May be partially accountable for past product woes at Zalora and Accenture Finland.

[SECOND TALK]
Ridzwan Aminuddin from Ninjavan

"Slaying the API beast - for Product Managers"

As product guys we often have to work with APIs, yet many of us aren't even familiar with the concept of an API much less how to work with engineers who speak API. This session is meant to provide you with a high level overview of APIs and equip you with the fundamentals suitable for product managers and empower you to decipher and analyse common api document formats.

Ridzwan Aminuddin is an entrepreneurial and passionate product leader with deep experience in mobile, web, digital data and e-commerce. He has over 10 years experience leading product and technology teams to carry out large-scale, complex consumer/commercial enterprise products and services. Ridzwan is currently the head product guy at Ninja Van, and is focussed on building a kickass product team with world-class product practices to disrupt last-mile logistics in South East Asia. His favourite quote in product management is “A great product manager has the brain of an engineer, the heart of a designer, and the speech of a diplomat.
