For patients to share life saving information such as drug allergy and blood type to relevant personnel for emergency situation such as severe allergy or car crash requiring transfusion. Medication error reduction is one of the top WHO's patient safety goals. Preliminary idea is to use the credit card concept to access medical info. Essentially to carry a critical medical info card in your apple wallet or google pay. The healthcare worker could use a cc reader to access your files
