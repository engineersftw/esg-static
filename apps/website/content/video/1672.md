Do not leave your unlocked computers unattended! One should take this advice seriously at the office of the Singapore Power’s Digital Team. Some malicious people in this department are quick to pounce on your unlocked systems to embarrass you if you leave your machine unlocked. Find out how Kheng Meng defended against this threat with a hardware device and a Swift desktop app.
