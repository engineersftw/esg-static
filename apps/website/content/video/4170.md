This event is brought to you by GoSEA, a collaboration among three Go communities across Southeast Asia - Go Singapore, Go Kuala Lumpur, and Go Jakarta, to discuss the latest tools, use cases, and applications of Golang.

Name: Chua Bok Woon
Topic: A Type-safe Query Builder and Struct Mapper for Go

Bok Woon studies Computer Engineering at National University of Singapore. In this talk, he shows how the sq query builder library can make your SQL queries in Go type-safe, compose programmatically and boilerplate free. Find out more at https://github.com/bokwoon95/go-structured-query/.
