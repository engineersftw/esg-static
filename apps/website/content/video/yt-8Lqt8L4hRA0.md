As AI becomes embedded in every product, traditional QA approaches are no longer enough. AI-based systems behave probabilistically, learn from imperfect data, and degrade over time.

This talk explores three core challenges:

• Non-determinism (same input, different output)

• Bias (models inheriting human and data flaws)

• Model drift (performance decay over time)

Solution: The AI testing 4R framework (Reliability, Robustness, Responsibility, Resilience)
