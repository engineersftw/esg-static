Speaker: Trung Vo, Engineering Manager, Ascenda
LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/trungvose

Have you ever watched a software engineer who moves effortlessly through their code and thought, “I wish I could code and browse that fast!”? There’s something almost mesmerizing about the speed and precision of a keyboard pro, like the soothing rhythm of an ASMR video. It’s not just about speed; it’s about making your workflow smooth and efficient. In this talk, I’ll guide you through the secrets of keyboard mastery, from ditching the mouse to measuring effectiveness with KLM. You’ll pick up a ton of tips for navigating VS Code with finesse—moving the cursor, selecting, sorting, and accessing files in a flash. We’ll also explore clever tricks like alias commands, browsing shortcuts, and using Alfred. By the end, you’ll have a fresh set of tools to make your coding and browsing more enjoyable and efficient.
