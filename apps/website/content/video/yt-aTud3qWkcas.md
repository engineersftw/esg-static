Problem Statement:
Modern teams ship fast, but UI quality doesn't scale.

Designs approved in Figma often don't match what reaches production.
Selector-based UI tests are fragile, slow to maintain, and flaky.
Manual visual checks don't scale across web, mobile, native apps, and PDFs.
The result: more test code, less coverage, late feedback, and missed UI defects.

Solution:
You—the living, breathing, thinking human—are more important than ever. AI scales visual validation across the SDLC, removing repetitive, error-prone work so you can focus on judgment, risk, and delivering real UI quality—faster and with confidence.
