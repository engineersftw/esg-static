Deployment of Omnichannel B2B2C Commerce, Order Management & Logistics Platforms

Speaker: Vijay Pagadala

Vijay is Chief Strategy Officer (CSO) at Ranosys Technologies.

The topic will focus on commerce providers, wholesalers, distributors and retailers in all industry segments and how they can leverage on integrated B2B2C platforms to streamline and fine tune their product offerings, sales processes, customer service, order management, last mile delivery & logistics. The topic will discuss deployment trends geared towards optimal utilisation of their valuable assets and resources to achieve robust growth and financial viability.
