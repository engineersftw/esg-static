Speaker: Richard Neil Ilagen, AWS

Container clusters --- Kubernetes included --- are notorious for becoming incredibly complicated to manage incredibly fast. In this session, we'll introduce and talk about GitOps, a popular approach in managing your clusters in a scalable, secure, and often hands-free manner. Borrowing concepts from IaC and software engineering practices, GitOps allows you to manage your resources much like how you manage your application code.
