Send SMS in bulk without backend integration. This has been requested by researchers and MSWs who want a better way to communicate with the people they serve. Specific use cases: contact patients for lost & found items left in the bedside lockers, remind patients who are forgetful to bring in specific items for appointment to reduce nursing admin burdens and reduce rescheduling rates, and discharge planning.
