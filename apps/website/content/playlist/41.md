Mozilla Developer Roadshow events are fun, informative sessions for people who build the Web. Over the past nine months we’ve held 50 events all over the world sharing the word about the latest in Mozilla and Firefox technologies. In September, the DevRoadshow team was in Asia with the goals of finding local experts and connecting the community. Some of our most successful moments have been when we were able to bring local event organizers together to forge lasting relationships. Our first Asia event was in Singapore at the PayPal headquarters on September 19. Visit https://hacks.mozilla.org/2017/02/devroadshow/ for a full list of the cities.
